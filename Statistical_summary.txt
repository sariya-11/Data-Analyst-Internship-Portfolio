                      "STATISTICAL VALIDATION"

Objective:
    To validate customer purchasing behaviour using statistical testing and support analytical findings with data-driven evidence.
Hypothesis:
    Null Hypothesis (H0): Customer average purchase amount has no significant variation.
    Alternative Hypothesis (H1): Customer average purchase amount shows significant variation.
Statistical Method Used:
    Independent Sample T-Test
    Confidence Level: 95%
    Significance Level (Alpha): 0.05
Output:
    T Statistic: -0.16854996561581098
    P Value: 0.8703341093371598
Decision:
    Since the P Value is greater than 0.05, "Accept H0" (Null Hypothesis)
Interpretation:
    The statistical test indicates that there is no statistically significant difference observed between the selected customer purchase groups.
    The observed variation is not strong enough to reject the null hypothesis.
Conclusion:
    Statistical validation supported the analytical findings and confirmed that customer purchase behaviour remained statistically consistent between the selected groups.
    This process improved confidence in business decision-making and demonstrated the practical application of hypothesis testing in data analysis.
