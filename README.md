                   "Task 4 – Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation"
## Project Overview:
This task focused on transforming analytical outputs into meaningful business insights through storytelling and statistical validation.
The purpose of this project was not only to analyze data but also to communicate findings effectively and support business decision-making using statistical methods.
Data storytelling combines analytical results with business understanding to present information in a structured and actionable way.

## Objective:
The main objective of this task was to:
- Interpret analytical results obtained from previous tasks
- Convert raw findings into business insights
- Perform statistical validation using hypothesis testing
- Present results in a professional and understandable format
- Improve data-driven decision making

## Project Workflow:
### Step 1 – Understanding Dataset
Reviewed the dataset and observations generated during Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
### Step 2 – Dashboard Analysis
Used Power BI dashboards to identify:
- Customer purchase behaviour
- Distribution of spending
- Business trends
- Revenue patterns
### Step 3 – Data Storytelling
Converted analytical findings into business-focused insights.
Storytelling included:
- Problem identification
- Analytical observations
- Business interpretation
- Recommendations
### Step 4 – Statistical Validation
Performed Independent Sample T-Test using Python.
Hypothesis:
H0:
Customer average purchase amount has no significant variation.
H1:
Customer average purchase amount shows significant variation.
## Statistical Results:
Output:
Hypothesis Testing-
T Statistic: -0.16854996561581098
P Value: 0.8703341093371598
Decision:
Accept H0

INTERPRETATION:
    Since the p-value is greater than 0.05, there is no statistically significant difference observed between the selected customer purchase groups.
