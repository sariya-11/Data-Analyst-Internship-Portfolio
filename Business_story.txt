                      "CUSTOMER PURCHASE ANALYSIS – BUSINESS STORY"

OBJECTIVE:
    To analyze customer purchasing behaviour and identify patterns that improve business decision making.
BACKGROUND:
    The dataset was explored and cleaned in previous tasks. Dashboard analysis was completed to identify customer trends and
    purchasing patterns.
ANALYSIS:
 1.Customer Spending-
    Purchase amounts showed noticeable variation across customers.
 2.Customer Segmentation-
    Customers can be categorized into:
      *High spenders
      *Moderate spenders
      *Low spenders
 3.Purchase Trends-
    Certain locations and customer groups contributed more to revenue generation.
 4.Dashboard Findings-
    Interactive visualizations revealed:
     *Total purchase distribution
     *Average customer spending
     *Regional trends
RECOMMENDATION:
    The company should focus marketing efforts on high-value customers while improving engagement among moderate spenders.
CONCLUSION:
    Data-driven decisions can improve customer retention and business growth.
